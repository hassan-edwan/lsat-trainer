# Text Format Example
## Section: LR | 35
1. Pharmacist: Patients who take the new allergy medication report fewer
symptoms than patients who take the older one. So the new medication is more
effective.
Which one of the following, if true, most seriously weakens the pharmacist's argument?
(A) The new medication costs more than the older one.
(B) Patients who take the new medication were, on average, experiencing milder
allergies before they began treatment.
(C) Some patients take both medications.
(D) The older medication has been available for many years.
(E) Both medications are taken once per day.

2. All members of the debate club are juniors. Mara is not a junior.

Which one of the following must be true on the basis of the statements above?
(A) Mara is not a member of the debate club.
(B) Mara is a senior.
(C) Some juniors are not members of the debate club.
(D) Every junior is a member of the debate club.
(E) Mara is a member of some other club.

## Section: RC | 35 | Reading Comprehension
### Passage 1
Some historians argue that the printing press transformed scholarship mainly
by making texts cheaper. Others emphasize standardization: identical copies
let scholars across Europe cite the same page.
1. The passage is primarily concerned with
(A) contrasting two accounts of the printing press's effect on scholarship
(B) arguing that printed books were cheaper
(C) describing how pages were numbered
(D) criticizing historians of science
(E) explaining how printing presses worked
2. According to the passage, standardization allowed scholars to
(A) cite the same page across copies
(B) buy cheaper texts
(C) avoid errors entirely
(D) print their own books
(E) read faster

## Answers
B A
A A
